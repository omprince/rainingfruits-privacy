Raining Fruits – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Raining Fruits ("the app", "the game") is a casual fruit game for Android, suitable for children and families. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. We keep it simple: we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

1. Information that stays on your device

The game saves the following only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server we operate:

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars and high scores.
  • Player names you type in Continuous mode, and the scores for each name. You can use a nickname; no real name is needed.
  • Your settings (music, sound effects, voice and vibration on or off, volume).

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the menu and level-map screens, and occasional full-screen or optional rewarded video ads (for example, to earn an extra heart). Ads are never shown while you are playing a level.

Because the app is intended for use by children, we configure every ad request as follows:

  • Treated as child-directed and for users under the age of consent, in line with COPPA, GDPR and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • Ad content limited to the G (General audiences) maximum rating.
  • No personalized advertising and no interest-based ads.
  • The app does not use or request the Android Advertising ID (the AD_ID permission is removed).

To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process technical information such as your IP address, device and OS type, and diagnostic or performance data. We do not receive or control this data. Google's handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Internet access

The app uses the internet only to load ads. The game itself works offline.

4. Permissions

  • Internet: to load ads.
  • Vibrate: a short buzz when a life is lost (can be switched off in Settings).

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or location.

5. Voice and text-to-speech

Fruit names are spoken using audio clips bundled with the app. If a clip is missing, the app may use Android's built-in text-to-speech engine on your device. The app does not record audio.

6. Children's privacy

Raining Fruits is designed to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob, configured for child-directed treatment as described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Game data lives in your device's private app storage, protected by Android's app sandbox.

8. Your choices

  • Delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Turn off vibration, voice and sound in the app's Settings.
  • Parents can manage ad and privacy options for a child's device in Google Play settings and Google Family Link.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes, such as adding a new third-party service, will be reflected here before they take effect.
